getting here

Travel & stay

Comrie Croft sits just outside Comrie in Perthshire, about 90 minutes from Edinburgh or Glasgow. A few options depending on how you're arriving.

By car

Free on-site parking. Enter Comrie Croft into Google maps, the address is Braincroft, Crieff PH7 4JZ.

By train

Gleneagles station is around 25 minutes away by taxi, with direct trains from Edinburgh and Glasgow. There will be plenty of cars making there way from Glasgow so let us know if you are strapped for a lift

Staying over

There are many local accommodation options alongside the opportunity to camp — see our full list just below — or ask me and Kasia for help.

Getting between venues

Everything happens on the one site, so once you're in, you're in for the day. There is plenty of parking on site.

staying nearby

Accommodation

A few options nearby, from cosy and budget-friendly to a little bit special. Book early — Comrie gets busy!

Fordie Estate

Our lovely neighbours, with lodges and cottages — the nearest alternative (luxury) accommodation, most of it a short and pleasant walk from the Croft. Their cottages are all on one level, so great for any elderly relatives who'd like to be nearby but not in amongst everything.

Ben Hennie & Geordie's Byre

More beautiful cottages, located just outside Comrie.

Dundas Flat, Comrie

A beautifully decorated 1 bedroom apartment in Comrie, just 3 miles along the road.

The Gables

Tucked away, just a one minute walk to the Comrie main street — 3 miles along the road from the Croft.

Leven House

A Victorian townhouse only a 2 minute walk from Crieff High Street and 4.5 miles from Comrie Croft. Five en-suite rooms, plus an annexed self-catered apartment (Squirrel Away) with 2 en-suite bedrooms — they can cater for larger exclusive-use groups of 11–15.

Caban Dubh

A dreamy hideaway in St Fillans, 7 miles along the road.

Huts at Cultybraggan Prisoner of War Camp

Every building tells a story, and this one certainly has a rich history — five beautifully modernised Nissen huts, part of the former Cultybraggan POW camp, just a mile outside Comrie.

The Royal Hotel

A lovely hotel in Comrie, very handy if you're just needing a one night stay.

Newcroft Comrie

3 bed, self catering, 3 miles along the road in Comrie.

Monzie Estate

For guests looking for something a little special, quiet and luxurious.

Loch Monzievaird

Lodges and cottages for hire, just a few minutes along the road from the Croft.

Luxury @ The Bothy and budget @ The Den

Unique places to stay in rural Perthshire (St Fillans, 7 miles away), bookable on Airbnb, open all year. They can also offer early check-in and late check-out.

The Indie House

For guests looking for something quiet and luxurious.

Bracken Hill Cottage

Just along the road — a hot tub to melt your stresses away and a woodburning stove to curl up by. Pet-friendly, ideal for families and friends.

Blairmore Farm

Just 11 miles away — six luxury cottages sleeping from 2 to 10 people, including one that's fully wheelchair accessible.

Crieff Hydro

Lots to do at the hydro — why not make a weekend of it!

The Meadow Inn, Crieff

Rooms in Crieff, a few minutes from the Croft.

The Hoolit

A cosy cottage option in the area.

The Tower Crieff

A characterful place to stay in Crieff.
